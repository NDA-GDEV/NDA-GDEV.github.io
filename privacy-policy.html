Privacy Policy for Warp Mania

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when using Warp Mania.

By installing or using Warp Mania, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Developer Information

Warp Mania is developed and published by Nathan Dascotte.

Information We Collect

To provide online functionality, analytics, advertising, and in-app purchases, Warp Mania and its third-party service providers may collect certain information, including:

  • Player identifiers generated by Unity Gaming Services.
  • Google Play Games account identifiers and display names.
  • Leaderboard information such as player names and scores.
  • Device information (device model, operating system, language, region).
  • Gameplay and usage analytics.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising performance data.
  • Diagnostic and crash information.
  • Information related to in-app purchases.

How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes:

  • Authenticate players.
  • Provide online services and leaderboard functionality.
  • Synchronize game-related data.
  • Improve game performance, stability, and user experience.
  • Analyze gameplay and application usage.
  • Serve and measure advertisements.
  • Process in-app purchases.

Third-Party Services

Warp Mania uses third-party services that may collect and process data according to their own privacy policies.

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Leaderboards, Analytics)
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Unity LevelPlay (ironSource)
  • Google AdMob
  • Unity Ads

Advertising

Warp Mania displays advertisements through Unity LevelPlay and its advertising partners.

Advertising providers may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP addresses, and technical information necessary to deliver, optimize, and measure advertisements.

Users may manage advertising preferences through their device settings.

In-App Purchases

Warp Mania offers optional in-app purchases processed through Google Play Billing.

Payment information is processed directly by Google and is not stored by the developer.

Data Sharing

The developer does not sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by the third-party services listed above solely for the purpose of operating, maintaining, improving, and monetizing the Game.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

Children's Privacy

Warp Mania is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by local regulations.

If you believe that personal information from a child has been collected unintentionally, please contact us so appropriate action can be taken.
